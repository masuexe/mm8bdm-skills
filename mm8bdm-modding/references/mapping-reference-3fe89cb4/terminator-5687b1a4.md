# Terminator

> ⚡ actor TerminatorStart 6000

## Usage

---

`"Terminator Capsule Start"` is a Thing located under the category `MM8BDM-TermPoss`. This object is used to create spawns unique to the Terminator Capsule in Terminator.

> 💡 **Warning: If you’re using a Zandronum configuration in Ultimate Doom Builder, this Thing may appear underneath the **`Powerups`** category instead.**

By default, the Terminator Capsule that appears in the Terminator game mode will copy the Deathmatch starts of a map. However, the Terminator Capsule fails to spawn properly at certain Deathmatch starts such as ones on top of 3D floors. 

In this case, it may be desirable to specify alternate or narrowed down locations which the Terminator Capsule will use instead. The existence of a single Terminator Capsule start will disallow it from using any of the Deathmatch starts.

> 💡 **Tip: In gameplay, the Terminator Capsule gives a huge boost of defenses, HP restore, and increased firepower. Although you may still want to have multiple Terminator Capsule starts, you may want to put them in precarious or risky positions to treat it as a powerful item.**
