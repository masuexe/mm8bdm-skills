# Teleport Entrance

> ⚡ actor MM8BDM\_TeleportEntrance : SecActHitFloor 10562

## Usage

---

`"Teleport Entrance"` is a Thing located under the category `MM8BDM-Mapper Tools`. This object is used to create teleporters.

To create a teleporter, put this actor into the sector you wish to teleport from, place a [Teleport Destination](https://zdoom.org/wiki/Classes:TeleportDest) where you want it to exit, give the Teleport Destination a tag, and then place that tag into the `Destination` field under the `Action / Tag / Misc.` tab of the placed `“Teleport Entrance”`.

### Action and Identification

- Argument 1: `Destination` - The tag of the Teleport Destination.
