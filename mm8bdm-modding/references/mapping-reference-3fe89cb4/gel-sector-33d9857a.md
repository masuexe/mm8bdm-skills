# Gel Sector

---

`"Gel Sector"` is a Thing located under the category `MM8BDM-Mapper Tools`. This object is used to create chemical gel that pulls players who fall in towards the top, like the purple pools on Burst Man’s stage.

To create gel in your map, simply place this actor down, click the `Action / Tag / Misc.` tab, and fill in the necessary info.

### Action and Identification

- Tag: `Tag` - Which tag to apply the gel to. 0 Applies the gel to all tags and prevents other sectors from being created.

> 💡 **Tip: Gel sector building always builds downwards. The actor’s z-position is the starting point, and anything below that within the **`Tag`** is considered “in the gel.” Players in there will begin to float upwards until they hit the surface of the gel.**
